Project 2025 × Current Events

A cross-compare of the Heritage Foundation's "Mandate for Leadership" (30 chapters) against what has actually happened as of November 15, 2026 — and what's most likely coming through 2027, with household prep notes.
Context that changed everything this month: the Nov 3, 2026 midterms swept Democrats into full control of Congress (House ≈256–169, Senate ≈53–44 +2 independents, per AP/NPR tallies as of Nov 15). That means from January 2027, new Project 2025-style legislation is largely dead on arrival — but executive orders, agency rules, and court battles continue, and several already-passed laws (OBBBA work requirements, SNAP cost-shift) are scheduled to bite regardless of who holds Congress. Sources: NPR · AP

Where the whole playbook stands

Independent trackers (Project 2025 Tracker / Yardstick) estimate roughly 53–78% of the 321 published objectives are completed or in progress (community tracker: 141 done, 70 in progress, 110 not started). The mandate itself is 30 chapters; below is each major chapter cross-compared with current events. Sources: Project 2025 Tracker · Heritage chapter list

Ch. 3 — Civil Service "Schedule F" Partial · in court

  • Playbook: reclassify tens of thousands of career federal workers as at-will political appointees.
  • Now: Day-one EO 14171 recreated it as "Schedule Policy/Career"; June 2026 EO ordered transfers to begin. NTEU lawsuit is live (motions pending Oct 2026); no merits ruling yet.
  • Household angle: the staff who process Social Security, tax returns, and benefits are harder to reach — expect slower federal help lines to persist.

Ch. 11 — Education (eliminate the Dept.) Partial · in court

  • Playbook: abolish the department, devolve to states, end Title IX gender protections, expand vouchers, kill PSLF.
  • Now: March 2025 EO; ~half the staff RIF'd; SCOTUS let layoffs proceed (July 2025) but the department legally still exists. Pell/FAFSA checks keep flowing. Student loans slated to move to a new entity by ~Oct 2027 (contested plan).
  • Household angle: slower civil-rights complaints, grant cancellations, borrower confusion — no interruption of aid so far.

Ch. 5 — DHS / Immigration Largely implemented

  • Playbook: mass deportations, border sealing, end "catch and release."
  • Now: FY2026 records — 491k+ ICE arrests, 483k deportations, funded by ~$170B from OBBBA; 2,500+ local 287(g) deals. Birthright-citizenship EO struck down 6–3 by SCOTUS (June 30, 2026); a narrower second EO is in litigation.
  • Household angle: if you know anyone in a mixed-status family, the practical reality is pervasive fear and family separations by attrition — this was a top driver of the Democratic midterm sweep.

Ch. 14 — HHS / Healthcare Partial · 2027 deadlines

  • Playbook: reorient HHS around "life from conception," defund reproductive care, block-grant Medicaid.
  • Now: Medicaid 80-hr/month work requirements start Jan 1, 2027 (CBO: ~5.3M could lose coverage); a judge vacated the narrowed "medical frailty" exemption test (Oct 2026) and CMS says states may delay disenrollment to July 2027. Mifepristone: 5th Cir. tried to ban telehealth/mail, SCOTUS stayed it (May 2026) — access preserved while litigation continues. This is the biggest unresolved flashpoint.

Ch. 10 — Agriculture / SNAP Implemented & expanding

  • Playbook: stricter SNAP work requirements and eligibility limits.
  • Now: OBBBA cut SNAP ~$190B; ~5M people (13%) already dropped — enrollment lowest in ~two decades. A Nov 2026 USDA proposed rule would end broad-based categorical eligibility, cutting ~6M more plus ~2M kids from free school meals (effective ~2029). Oct 2027: states must start paying 5–15% of benefit costs (several states already suing/fretting).
  • Household angle: if SNAP/food stamps touch anyone in your orbit, recertification paperwork is where people get tripped — the #1 realistic loss mechanism is red tape, not policy.

Ch. 27 — CFPB Blocked by courts

  • Playbook: abolish the consumer protection bureau.
  • Now: Vought's shutdown push (layoffs, defunding) repeatedly ruled unlawful; courts ordered $145M in funds restored; agency survived but enforcement is sharply narrower and complaint intake degraded.
  • Household angle: less federal backup on bank/credit-card disputes — keep your own paper trails, and lean on state consumer-protection offices.

Ch. 22/24 — Treasury, Fed, Tax Tax law passed

  • Playbook: make TCJA cuts permanent, shrink IRS, restrict the Fed.
  • Now: OBBBA (July 2025) made TCJA individual cuts permanent — the feared 2026 "sunset" never happened. For 2027: standard deduction $16,600/$33,200, Child Tax Credit up to $2,300. IRS cut ~28–30k staff; Direct File killed; refunds still paid but help lines are weeks-long waits. Paper checks now take ~36 days.

Ch. 26 — Trade / Tariffs Blocked, replaced

  • Playbook: aggressive tariffs (Navarro chapter).
  • Now: SCOTUS struck down the IEEPA "Liberation Day" tariffs (Feb 2026); Trump replaced them with Section 301/232 tariffs — effective rate ~7.1% (Aug 2026), highest sustained in decades. ~$100B+ refunded via CBP.
  • Household angle: roughly $1,000+/yr embedded cost on typical consumption; prices keep drifting up on imported goods.

Ch. 13/12 — EPA / Energy Partial · rules pending

  • Playbook: deregulate, expand fossil fuels, kill EV/renewable programs.
  • Now: 2026 Unified Agenda shows EPA reconsidering MY2027+ vehicle emissions standards; DOE revising EV CAFE math. NOAA climate work narrowed; NCAR dismantlement partially enjoined.

Ch. 18 — Labor Partial

  • Playbook: withdraw overtime, joint-employer, and union-protection rules.
  • Now: overtime and union rules rolled back via rulemaking and litigation; tip/overtime tax deductions run only through 2028 (OBBBA).

DEI / Ch. 29 — Election law Mixed in court

  • Now: DEI removal largely implemented (17 EOs, $7.3B claimed cut, 90% of S&P 500 trimmed programs); contractor EO upheld narrowly, some school directives vacated. SAVE Act died in the Senate; USPS voting restrictions blocked through the midterms — but post-midterm bulk voter-list purges using SAVE are expected under the new divided Congress's fights.

Ch. 10/14 — FDA / MAHA food policy Rolling out

  • Now: Red Dye No. 3 compliance Jan 15, 2027; petroleum dye phase-out by end of 2027 (voluntary); GRAS "self-affirmed additive" loophole closure proposed Aug 2026; first federal ultra-processed-food definition under review; front-of-package labeling rule due Dec 2026.
  • Household angle: gradual reformulation on shelves — mild, mostly benign changes; whole milk back in schools.

Not implemented / dead for now

  • NIH block-grant restructuring, NIST/USPTO merger, DOJ "China Initiative" relaunch — not started. NIH/NSF 15% indirect-cost cap permanently struck down (1st Cir., Jan 2026), old methodology locked into law. Pornography-criminalization bill still in committee — and with a Democratic Congress, going nowhere.

Most likely coming up — the timeline

January 1, 2027
Medicaid work requirements begin (80 hrs/month, expansion adults in 44 states + GA/WI partial-expansion). Disenrollment can be delayed to July 2027 per CMS. Highest-impact item for households on Medicaid
January 2027
Democratic Congress seated — expect clashes over spending, investigations, and attempts to delay the SNAP state cost-shift; expect government-shutdown brinkmanship (a realistic 2027 event to plan cash around).
Jan–Apr 2027
Inflation likely peaks (Allianz: CPI ~4.3% early 2027, 3.3% avg; S&P: easing to 2.3% by end). US Bank expects the Fed to raise rates to 4.25–4.50%. Grocery forecast +1% (wide error bars on beef/eggs).
Jan 15, 2027
Red Dye 3 out of foods; front-of-package labeling rule lands Dec 2026.
2027 plan year
Health insurance premiums jump again — ACA benchmark silver ~+13% (≈$704/mo for a 40-yr-old), employer costs +8–11% (largest in ~20 yrs). Enhanced ACA subsidies already expired, so above-400%-FPL households eat the full increase.
Spring–June 2027
SCOTUS decisions from the Oct 2026 term (climate torts Suncor v. Boulder, veterans' benefits, jury size Kian v. Florida), plus likely rulings on Schedule F, mifepristone/Comstock, and the second birthright-citizenship EO as petitions ripen. June–July 2027 is when those land.
October 1, 2027
SNAP state cost-shift begins (states pay 5–15% of benefits, ~$9B yr one). Congress under Democrats may try to delay it — watch the news, but if you or family rely on SNAP, don't count on the delay.
October 2027
Student loans possibly moved out of the Education Dept. to a new entity (contested plan) — if anyone in the family is on an income-driven plan, recheck servicer info after any transition.
Late 2027 → 2029
Immigration: H-1B fee fight continues ($100k fee extended to Sept 2027 but blocked in court), wage-weighted lottery live, H-4 work authorization at risk. Food: USDA categorical-eligibility rule would cut ~6M from SNAP ~2029 if it survives the comment period and courts.

Plain-English prep notes for our household

  • Healthcare is the big one. Anyone on Medicaid should treat Jan 2027 as a paperwork deadline: know your state's reporting portal, keep pay stubs/hours documentation, and watch for renewal notices (most losses will be missed-paperwork, not policy). Budget for higher premiums in the 2027 plan year regardless of employer vs. marketplace.
  • Food & household budget: tariffs and food inflation mean the $1,000+/yr embedded hit continues into early 2027 before easing. Stocking the pantry slowly before the late-2027 SNAP shifts is cheap insurance; food banks are already stretched.
  • Taxes: 2027 brings a bigger standard deduction and $2,300 CTC — file early (IRS help is slow), and don't wait on paper checks (36-day waits). Direct File is gone; free options are now state/private tools only.
  • Money safety nets are thinner: CFPB enforcement is degraded, so dispute card charges fast, in writing, and keep records; use Georgia consumer-protection resources as backup.
  • Shutdown watch: with a Democratic Congress and this White House, funding fights are near-certain — a government shutdown sometime in 2027 is a live risk (benefits delays hit contractors and SNAP timing first).
  • Elections: post-midterm voter-roll maintenance using the SAVE database is expected in states — if anyone in your circle has a name/document mismatch (marriage, naturalization), verify registration before 2027 local elections.
